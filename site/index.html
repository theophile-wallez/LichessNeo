Elo as on Chess.com Experimental

Ever wondered what your rating would be on Chess.com? Turn this on and every Elo on Lichess shows its Chess.com twin: in games, on profiles, on leaderboards. Hover one to see the original.

Based on a study of 35,515 players with a rating on both sites.

See how much Elo you’ll win or lose!

Every rated game shows it for a win, a draw or a loss, counted just like Lichess does. The stronger your opponent, the bigger the prize!

Pick your coach

A fennec, a dragon, a frog… pick the one you like! It’ll go over your games with you, cheer you on during puzzles and tell you what each lesson is about.

Made the Lichess way

Lichess is free, open and fair, and so is everything Lichess Neo adds.

Free, forever
No ads, no sign-up, no paywall. Ever.
Your data stays yours
No account, no tracking. Your settings live in your browser.
Open source
Every line of code is on GitHub, under the MIT license.
Fair play
No engine while you’re playing. Your review waits until the game is over.
