It’s still Lichess. Your account, your games and your friends stay as they are. Only the look and the sounds change, and a coach comes along.

A review for every game

Open any finished game on lichess.org: the review is there, free, worked out in your browser.

The summary

Each player’s accuracy, the rating they played like, their moves by class and each phase’s verdict. Here, the Opera Game’s: click a count to see its move.

The advantage graph marks each turning point.

The coach explains each move

What the move did, the better one when there was, and a chance to find it yourself on the board.

A coach by your side

Twenty-six of them, in 3D, from a fennec to a dragon. Yours reviews your games, cheers you on in puzzles and reads you the lessons’ goals. It can be your avatar too.

Ratings as on Chess.com Experimental

Turn it on, and every rating on Lichess shows as it would be on Chess.com: in games, on profiles, in lists and leaderboards. Point at one for its Lichess rating.

Converted from a study of 35,515 players with an established rating on both sites.
