# Fonts

- `inter.woff2`: [Inter](https://github.com/rsms/inter) 4.1, the page's type, subset to Latin,
  under the SIL Open Font License (`OFL.txt`).
- `lichess-chess.woff2`: the font Lichess draws its move list's figurines in, by the
  [pgn4web](http://pgn4web.casaschi.net/home.html) authors, under the
  [GPLv2+](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-2.0.txt), copied from
  [lila](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/font).
- `lichess-modes.woff2`: Lichess's icon font cut to the four speeds' glyphs (bullet, blitz,
  rapid, classical) with fontTools' `pyftsubset`, copied from
  [lila](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/font), by the authors and
  under the licenses lila's
  [COPYING.md](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/master/COPYING.md) lists for it
  (OFL, MIT, CC BY 3.0, AGPLv3+).
