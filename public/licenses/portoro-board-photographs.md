# The Portoro board's photographs

`img/boards/portoro.webp` is the one texture in the extension that is not
drawn. It is cut out of five photographs of a single real marble chess board,
square by square, by `tools/board-materials/photo.py`. Every other board
texture is drawn from seeded noise.

Those photographs were supplied by this project's author, who holds the rights
to use them. They are in neither the extension nor the repository: what crossed
over is the 64 squares cut from them, in that one file.

Nothing here comes from another chess site, redrawn, traced or otherwise (see
the product rules in `AGENTS.md`).
