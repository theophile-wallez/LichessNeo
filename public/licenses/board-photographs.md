# The board photographs

Ten of the board textures in `img/boards` are not drawn. Each reproduces one
real chess board and is cut square by square out of photographs of it, by
`tools/board-materials/photo.py`:

| Texture      | The real board                        |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| `burl-gloss` | glossed maple and walnut burl         |
| `burl`       | bird's eye maple and walnut burl      |
| `vavona`     | bird's eye maple and vavona burl      |
| `cocobolo`   | bird's eye maple and cocobolo         |
| `amboyna`    | bird's eye maple and amboyna burl     |
| `olmo`       | maple and elm burl                    |
| `padauk`     | bird's eye maple and padauk           |
| `palisander` | bird's eye maple and wenge            |
| `pomelle`    | curly maple and pomelle bubinga       |
| `portoro`    | white marble and Portoro black marble |

Those photographs were supplied by this project's author, who holds the rights
to use them. They are in neither the extension nor the repository: what crossed
over is the 64 squares cut from each, in those ten files. Every other texture is
drawn from seeded noise (`materials.py`) and comes from nothing but the code.

Nothing here comes from another chess site, redrawn, traced or otherwise (see
the product rules in `AGENTS.md`).
